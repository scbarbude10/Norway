The Lofoten Islands are a dramatic archipelago in Northern Norway, situated above the Arctic Circle. Renowned for their steep mountains rising directly from the sea, white sand beaches, and picturesque fishing villages like Reine and Henningsvær, they are a premier destination for hiking, photography, kayaking, and experiencing the Midnight Sun (summer) or Northern Lights (September–April).
